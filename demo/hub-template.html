Demo 1 · runs in your browser

The AI receptionist and the clinic's books

An invented clinic, Dr. Sen's Dental Clinic in Kolkata. You play the patient; it answers, books the visit into the diary and sends the reminder. The other tabs show the follow-ups, messages, review rules by country, the doctor's morning sheet and the services.

Open the receptionist demo claude.ai/artifact/Pk8LMPRGUZnJUJR86wxwjS
  1. Open it in Chrome on the tablet or a computer, or in the Claude desktop app. That is where it can listen and speak. In the Claude phone app you type instead.
  2. Check the badge at the top of the call. "Claude is answering" means the real AI receptionist. "Scripted stand-in" means a simple fallback is answering.
  3. Speak or type as a patient: "I have a toothache, can I come tomorrow evening?" Give a name and a number when it asks, and say yes to book. The first message asks your permission to use Claude.
  4. Open the Diary tab (on a phone, the tabs are above the call). Your booking is there, marked yellow.
  5. Press "Next morning". The system sends what is due by itself: the box at the top lists it, and the Messages tab shows every message.
  6. Reviews tab: switch the country between India and the United States to see the review request switch off and on.
  7. Daily sheet tab: what the doctor reads each morning. Reset starts again.

The first e-mail, two follow-ups (3 and 7 days later), and the reply that goes out with the brochure when a clinic answers "yes". Your name, business and number come from cg settings. The clinic names here are invented; on the tablet cg preview dental shows the same.
